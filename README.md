**The Door the Ocean Pours Through.** In a jungle clearing stands an impossible door — a giant, weathered wooden door, half-open on rusted hinges and tangled with vines — and instead of darkness or a path behind it, a full ocean wave pours OUT of the doorway, white foam bursting through the frame, curling up past the treetops and climbing into a bright blue sky. It lands in one glance because the contradiction is instant and complete: a door is meant to hold things in or keep them out, yet here the whole sea escapes through it, frozen mid-curl above the jungle floor.
